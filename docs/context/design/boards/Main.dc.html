Diagnosis

Eight stages, all weighted the same

Nothing here is missing. The problem is the shape. The path gives equal billing to stages that do nothing and stages that carry the whole job, and the answer the work exists to produce turns up second from last.

01
The best thing in the tool is framed as skipping the tool
“Process everything and show me what needs fixing” lives in a card inside stage 01, under the heading Skip the steps. That is the main road, labeled as a shortcut.
05
Fix problems holds nine things at once
A five number strip, two step tabs, the duplicate rules card, a cross-file note, ranked groups with diagnoses and lookups nested inside them, then the queue.
02
Two of the eight stages are usually pass-through
Set up your columns is read-only in Guided mode, so the person it was written for cannot act on it. Tidy up frequently says there is nothing to stage.
06
The answer turns up at stage 07
Unique clients, and unique clients by program, are why any of this exists. Six stages deep in column matching, there is nothing on screen to say so.
03
One question, asked in four places
Copies inside a file on Tidy up. Program names on Match. Row problems on Fix. The same person across files on Results. To a reviewer they are all “is this the same thing.”
07
Guided and Advanced is a mode, not a depth
Wanting one advanced control means relabelling the whole app. And Guided tells you to go and do that in the middle of the task it cannot finish.
04
The ledger is rebuilt four times
Process, Fix problems, Results and Download each restate the same row counts in different words and different groupings. The reader reconciles them by hand.
08
The menu appears when you rest on it and leaves when you move
Undo, Start over and Close Crosswalk share that same strip, so backing out one step and clearing the batch sit inches apart in a panel that is not always there.
One small thing worth fixing whatever we do: the stage headers read “Stage 04 of 07” while the rail lists eight. It is the kind of detail that quietly costs trust in every other number on the screen.
